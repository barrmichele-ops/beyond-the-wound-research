Aftercare and Emotional Support

Aftercare is part of the emotional support team. We meet patients and families during their inpatient stay, then follow them throughout recovery. The emotion-boosting experiences we hold on the unit and beyond it have purpose behind them. This research library explores the evidence connected to that work.
