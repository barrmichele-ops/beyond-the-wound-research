Various Authors

RelaxationBurns

Music Interventions in Burn Patients: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of RCTs
Various Authors
Burns · 2021
RelaxationBurns
10 RCTs, 1,061 participants: statistically significant reductions in pain and anxiety, and improved relaxation, all favoring music interventions over controls (p<0.001 for each). Music intervention began with burn patients in the late 1970s and has the strongest longitudinal evidence base of any single non-pharmacological relaxation approach in burn care.
